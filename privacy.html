Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Summary

Metrobüs Kaç Durak does not send your location or any other personal data to a server and does not share it with third parties. There are no accounts, logins, ads or tracking tools in the app. The one exception is anonymous usage statistics we collect to improve the app (see "Anonymous usage statistics" below); you can turn them off at any time in About. Counting stops works without an internet connection.

Location

The app uses your device's location during a trip to work out how many stops remain before the one you chose. That location is:

  • processed only on your device,
  • never sent to a server and never copied anywhere,
  • never shared with third parties, advertising networks or data brokers.

Granting location access is optional. If you decline, the app still works; use the Demo trip switch on the About screen to try the line without location. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

The app uses background location so it can keep counting stops while the screen is locked. This only happens after you start a trip and ends when you stop it.

Notifications

The app sends local notifications when you are 2 stops and 1 stop away from your destination. These notifications are generated on your device; no notification server is involved and no notification data is transmitted. If you decline notification permission, the rest of the app works normally.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps small records on your device to remember your choices:

  • your recent routes and selected stops,
  • settings such as language and the demo trip switch,
  • trip logs kept for troubleshooting (stop crossings, approximate location, app version and battery percentage).

These records stay on your device, inside the app's own folder, and at most the last 20 trips are kept. They are never uploaded automatically. If you use the Share button on About → Trip logs, the file goes to whichever app you pick (e.g. Mail) — that is an action you take yourself. Deleting the app deletes these records.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features are used and improve the app, we use Google's Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4). What is collected:

  • which screens are opened,
  • when a trip starts and ends; the names of the boarding and destination stops, direction, number of stops and trip length in minutes,
  • whether location permission had a problem (the type only),
  • app version, device model, iOS version, language and the approximate country/city Google derives from the connection (your IP address is not stored).

These statistics are kept under a random, app-specific identifier. It is not your advertising identifier (IDFA) and is never matched with your name, email or any other information. Your location, coordinates and trip logs are never included. The data is processed on Google's servers (it may be transferred abroad) and kept for at most 14 months.

If you prefer not to take part, turn off About → Anonymous usage statistics; from that moment nothing is sent.

Data we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health data, payment information or advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected. The app contains no advertising SDKs; the only analytics tool is Firebase Analytics, described above.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Usage statistics are not linked to any information that identifies you, so no record about you can be singled out for deletion or export; you can turn measurement off at any time. The app stores nothing else on a server. You can clear the records on your device by deleting the app or removing individual entries from About → Trip logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the "last updated" date above will change. If a change ever introduced data collection, it would also be announced inside the app.
