Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Metrobüs Kaç Durak does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, logins, ads, analytics or tracking tools in the app. The app does not use an internet connection while running.

Location

The app uses your device's location during a trip to work out how many stops remain before the one you chose. That location is:

  • processed only on your device,
  • never sent to a server and never copied anywhere,
  • never shared with third parties, advertising networks or data brokers.

Granting location access is optional. If you decline, the app still works; use the Demo trip switch on the About screen to try the line without location. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

The app uses background location so it can keep counting stops while the screen is locked. This only happens after you start a trip and ends when you stop it.

Notifications

The app sends local notifications when you are 2 stops and 1 stop away from your destination. These notifications are generated on your device; no notification server is involved and no notification data is transmitted. If you decline notification permission, the rest of the app works normally.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps small records on your device to remember your choices:

  • your recent routes and selected stops,
  • settings such as language and the demo trip switch,
  • trip logs kept for troubleshooting (stop crossings, approximate location, app version and battery percentage).

These records stay on your device, inside the app's own folder, and at most the last 20 trips are kept. They are never uploaded automatically. If you use the Share button on About → Trip logs, the file goes to whichever app you pick (e.g. Mail) — that is an action you take yourself. Deleting the app deletes these records.

Data we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health data, payment information, advertising identifier (IDFA) or usage statistics are collected. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since the app holds no data about you and stores nothing on a server, there is no record to delete or export. You can clear the records on your device by deleting the app or removing individual entries from About → Trip logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the "last updated" date above will change. If a change ever introduced data collection, it would also be announced inside the app.
