Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy applies to the Kaç Durak: Metrobüs, Metro app (formerly called Metrobüs Kaç Durak).

Summary

Kaç Durak does not send your location coordinates to a server and does not share them with third parties. There are no accounts, logins or ads in the app, and it contains no advertising SDKs. The only data that leaves the app is anonymous usage statistics sent to Google's Firebase Analytics to improve the app (see "Anonymous usage statistics" below). This measurement is on by default; you can turn it off at any time in About. Counting stops works without an internet connection.

Covered lines

The app shows how many stops are left before yours on the Metrobüs, Marmaray, metro (M1A, M1B, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9, M11) and tram (T1, T4, T5) lines. This policy is the same for all of them.

Location

The app uses your device's location during a trip to work out how many stops remain before the one you chose. That location is:

  • processed as coordinates only on your device,
  • never sent to a server as coordinates (for statistics only the name of the nearest stop, derived from your location, and a distance range are sent; see below),
  • never shared with third parties, advertising networks or data brokers.

Granting location access is optional. If you decline, the app still works; use the Demo trip switch on the About screen to try the app without location. You can revoke access at any time: on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, on Android in Settings → Apps → Kaç Durak → Permissions → Location.

Location in the background

The app keeps using location while the screen is locked or the app is in the background so it can keep counting stops. This only happens after you start a trip and ends when you stop it. If you forget to tap End, tracking stops by itself after 120 minutes without stop progress or once the trip passes 240 minutes in total; if the app is in the background, a short notification tells you. This way location is not used unnecessarily.

  • iPhone: during a trip the app uses iOS background location; the blue location indicator appears in the status bar.
  • Android: during a trip a foreground service of type "location" runs and a persistent notification is shown in the notification shade; both end when the trip ends. On Android the app does not ask for background location ("Allow all the time"); it only uses location while it is open or during a trip.

Tunnel lines and estimated counting

GPS does not work in tunnels, so on lines that run through tunnels (most metro lines and the tunnel section of Marmaray) the remaining stop count is estimated from the trip time; the estimate can be off by a stop or two. This calculation happens on your device. When the train departs you tap Train departed on screen; if the estimate drifts, you pick your current stop yourself with Fix my stop. If measurement is on, only the number of stops such a correction moved is sent, as part of the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Notifications

The app sends local notifications when you are 2 stops and 1 stop away from your destination, and on tunnel lines also based on the estimated count; on iPhone a "Has the train left?" reminder may also appear on tunnel lines. These notifications are generated and scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved and no notification data is transmitted. If you decline notification permission, the rest of the app works normally. On Android 13 and later, notification permission is requested separately inside the app. The persistent notification shown on Android during a trip is also generated on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Battery optimisation (Android)

Some Android phones (e.g. Xiaomi, Samsung) may stop the trip service to save battery. If that happens, the app may open the phone's App info screen (Battery → Unrestricted). Changing that setting is optional and does not share any data; it only helps tracking continue while the screen is locked.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps small records on your device to remember your choices:

  • your last selected line, recent routes and selected stops,
  • settings such as language, the demo trip switch and the anonymous statistics switch,
  • trip logs kept for troubleshooting: stop crossings, location measurements (latitude, longitude, accuracy and speed), when the app moved to the foreground or background, errors if any, events produced by the tunnel estimate, app version, platform and battery percentage.

Trip logs contain the measured locations as detailed coordinates. These records stay on your device, inside the app's own folder, and at most the last 20 trips are kept. They are never uploaded automatically. If you use the Share button on About → Trip logs, the file goes to whichever app you pick (e.g. Mail) — that is an action you take yourself, and you should know the file contains location before you share it. Deleting the app deletes these records.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features are used and improve the app, we use Google's Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4). Measurement is on by default. The events sent are:

  • which screens are opened (trip selection, tracking, arrival, About),
  • the selected line (e.g. Metrobüs, M2, T1) and its type (surface, partly tunnel, tunnel); the line is added to every event,
  • when a trip starts and ends: the names of the boarding and destination stops, direction, number of stops, completion percentage, how it ended (arrival or manual finish), where the boarding stop came from (nearest suggestion, recent route or your own choice) and trip length in minutes,
  • how a stop was chosen (search, list, nearest suggestion, recent route) and the name of the chosen stop,
  • whether location permission had a problem (the type only),
  • permission results (whether location and notification permission were granted or denied; only when the result changes),
  • technical information about alerts: how many stops were left when the alert came, whether it arrived while the app was open or in the background, whether notifications were enabled and whether the alert was based on the tunnel estimate,
  • the number of stops skipped during tracking, the number of stops remaining and whether the skip happened inside a tunnel,
  • tunnel estimate: that estimate mode started, by how many stops the estimate was ahead when location came back, and by how many stops Fix my stop changed the stop (numbers only),
  • the name of the nearest stop the app suggests as your boarding stop and a distance range (0-500 m, 500-1000 m, 1000-2000 m); your coordinates are not sent,
  • the text of a stop search that returned no results (lower-cased, up to 30 characters),
  • that the demo trip switch was turned on or off, a recent route was chosen, boarding and destination were swapped, and that trip logs were shared with Share (only how many logs, not their contents),
  • user properties: interface language, whether notifications are enabled and a range for how many trips you have made so far (0, 1, 2-4, 5+),
  • app version, device model, operating system (iOS or Android) version, language and the approximate country/city Google derives from the connection (your IP address is not stored).

During a demo trip the trip start, arrival and finish events are not sent. These statistics are kept under a random, app-specific identifier. It is not your advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android) and is never matched with your name, email or any other information. In the Android version the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) has been removed from the app; the advertising ID is not read or collected. Your coordinates and trip logs are never included. The data is processed on Google's servers (it may be transferred abroad) and kept for at most 14 months.

If you prefer not to take part, turn off About → Anonymous usage statistics; from that moment nothing is sent.

Data we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health data, payment information or advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android) is collected. The app contains no advertising SDKs; the only analytics tool is Firebase Analytics, described above. The app has no accounts or logins, so there is no account data either.

App stores

The app is distributed through the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android. Data that Apple or Google collect themselves when you download and update the app (e.g. your store account, download and crash reports) is subject to their own privacy policies and is outside our control: Apple, Google. After you finish your first trip, the app tries once to open the store's rating prompt; that prompt is shown by the store, and the app does not collect any review text you write.

Children

The app collects no child-specific data: no accounts are created and no name or contact details are taken. The anonymous usage statistics are the same for users of every age and can be turned off in About.

Your rights

Usage statistics are not linked to any information that identifies you, so no record about you can be singled out for deletion or export; you can turn measurement off at any time. The app stores nothing else on a server. You can clear the records on your device by deleting the app or removing individual entries from About → Trip logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the "last updated" date above will change. If a change ever introduced data collection, it would also be announced inside the app.
