Privacy & support

Kaç Durak: Metrobüs, Metro (formerly called Metrobüs Kaç Durak) is a free iPhone and Android app that shows how many stops are left before yours on Istanbul's Metrobüs, Marmaray, metro and tram lines. On tunnel lines the count is an estimate based on trip time.

Download on the App Store · Google Play — coming soon

Short version: no account, no ads. Your coordinates stay on your device. Only anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are collected; they are on by default and you can turn them off in About.
