Privacy & support

Metrobüs Kaç Durak is a free iPhone app that shows how many stops are left before yours on the Istanbul Metrobüs line.

Short version: no account, no ads. Your coordinates stay on your device. Only anonymous usage statistics are collected, and you can turn them off in About.
