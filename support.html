Support

Three taps to start: pick your boarding stop, pick your destination, tap Start. The remaining stop count appears in large type.

Can't find an answer? Email 1zaferyildiz@gmail.com. Including your iPhone model, iOS version and the stops you were travelling between helps a lot.

Trying it without being on the Metrobüs

Open the About screen with the (i) button at the top right and turn on the Demo trip switch. The app then ignores your real location and plays a sped-up fake Metrobüs along your chosen route. A yellow SİM badge appears on the selection screen. Remember to turn it off before a real trip.

Location permission and the "Allow" button

Counting stops requires location. Tapping Allow makes iOS ask for "While Using the App"; the app keeps using location for the duration of the trip, including while the screen is locked. If you declined by mistake, re-enable it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Metrobüs Kaç Durak, or use the Open Settings button inside the app.

Your location never leaves the device. See the privacy policy.

Notifications and the lock screen

  • You get a notification and a vibration at 2 stops and 1 stop before your destination.
  • Without notification permission the counter still works; you just won't be alerted while the screen is locked.
  • If alerts are silent, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Metrobüs Kaç Durak and enable Time Sensitive Notifications and sound. Focus mode can also mute them.

Common situations

  • "Waiting for location…" — GPS weakens in tunnels, underpasses and indoors. It recovers once you're in the open.
  • "Looks like you're off the line" — a location jump; if you're on the Metrobüs the counter recovers shortly.
  • "You may be heading the wrong way" — check the direction of your boarding and destination stops; use Swap direction on the selection screen.
  • The count advanced a stop early or late — near the bridge and junction points the two directions run close together, so this can rarely happen. Finishing and restarting the trip resets the counter.
  • Battery — the screen stays on and location is used during tracking, so the battery drains faster than usual on long trips. That's expected.

Trip logs

Each trip is logged on your device to help with troubleshooting (the last 20 trips at most). View them in About → Trip logs and send one yourself with Share. Logs never leave the device unless you share them.

About the app

  • Beylikdüzü – Söğütlüçeşme line, 44 stops, both directions.
  • No route planning and no live bus positions; it only counts the stops remaining.
  • No account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases; the app is free.
  • Turkish and English interface.
  • All processing happens on device; no internet needed.
