Support

Three taps to start: pick your boarding stop, pick your destination, tap Start. The remaining stop count appears in large type.

Can't find an answer? Email 1zaferyildiz@gmail.com. Including your phone model, iOS or Android version and the stops you were travelling between helps a lot.

Trying it without being on a trip

Open the About screen with the (i) button at the top right and turn on the Demo trip switch. The app then ignores your real location and plays a sped-up fake trip along your chosen route. A yellow SİM badge appears on the selection screen. Remember to turn it off before a real trip.

Location permission and the "Allow" button

Counting stops requires location. The app keeps using location for the duration of the trip, including while the screen is locked.

  • iPhone: tapping Allow makes iOS ask for "While Using the App". If you declined by mistake, re-enable it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Kaç Durak, or use the Open Settings button inside the app.
  • Android: tapping Allow makes Android ask for location access; choose "While using the app". "Allow all the time" is not needed. If you declined by mistake, go to Settings → Apps → Kaç Durak → Permissions → Location and choose Allow only while using the app.

Your location never leaves the device. See the privacy policy.

Notifications and the lock screen

  • You get a notification and a vibration at 2 stops and 1 stop before your destination.
  • Without notification permission the counter still works; you just won't be alerted while the screen is locked.
  • iPhone: if alerts are silent, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Kaç Durak and enable Time Sensitive Notifications and sound. Focus mode can also mute them.
  • Android: on Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission. If you declined, turn it on in Settings → Apps → Kaç Durak → Notifications. During a trip a persistent notification stays in the notification shade; it shows that tracking continues while the screen is locked and disappears when the trip ends.
  • Android, silent mode: when the phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb is on, the app still vibrates at 1 stop left and on arrival; no sound plays.

Android: battery optimisation

Some Android phones (especially Xiaomi, Samsung and similar) may stop the trip service to save battery; the counter then stops while the screen is locked and no alert arrives. If needed, the app opens the phone's App info screen; from there choose Battery → Unrestricted. This setting is optional.

  • General: Settings → Apps → Kaç Durak → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Samsung: same path, Battery → Unrestricted; if the app is in the "Sleeping apps" list, remove it.
  • Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: Settings → Apps → Metrobüs Kaç Durak → Battery saver: No restrictions and turn on Autostart.

Menu names and paths vary slightly by manufacturer and Android version.

Android: no notification on the lock screen

  • Check that notifications are allowed: Settings → Apps → Metrobüs Kaç Durak → Notifications. The Trip status, 2 stops left, Get off at the next stop and Arrival categories should be on. It is normal for the Location tracking category to look off or silent; it does not affect tracking.
  • In Settings → Notifications (Lock screen on some phones), make sure notifications are shown on the lock screen.
  • With Do Not Disturb or a similar mode on, alerts play no sound and may not appear on the lock screen; the vibration at 1 stop left and on arrival still comes.
  • If the persistent notification never appears during a trip, the phone may have stopped the service; apply the battery setting above and restart the trip.

Common situations

  • "Waiting for location…" — GPS weakens in tunnels, underpasses and indoors. It recovers once you're in the open. For how lines that run through tunnels work, see Tunnel lines below.
  • "Looks like you're off the line" — a location jump; if you're on board the counter recovers shortly.
  • "You may be heading the wrong way" — check the direction of your boarding and destination stops; use Swap direction on the selection screen.
  • The count advanced a stop early or late — near the bridge and junction points the two directions run close together, so this can rarely happen. Finishing and restarting the trip resets the counter.
  • Battery — during tracking the screen stays on while the app is open and location is used; if you lock the screen, tracking continues. The battery drains faster than usual on long trips. That's expected.
  • Tracking ended by itself — if you forget to tap End, tracking stops automatically after 120 minutes without stop progress or once the trip passes 240 minutes in total, so battery and location aren't wasted. If the app is in the background, a short "tracking stopped" notification appears.

Tunnel lines

  • Why is the count an estimate? GPS does not work in tunnels, so the app cannot see the train. On lines that run through tunnels (most metro lines, the tunnel section of Marmaray) the remaining stop count is estimated from schedule times and shown with an estimated label.
  • "Train departed": when the train moves, tap Train departed; the remaining stops are estimated from schedule times. If you don't tap it, you won't get alerts.
  • "Fix my stop": if the counter drifts from your real stop, tap Fix my stop and choose your current stop from the list; counting continues from there.
  • It can be off: if the train is delayed or runs fast, the estimate can be off by a stop or two. Check the stop name and the announcements before you get off.

Trip logs

Each trip is logged on your device to help with troubleshooting (the last 20 trips at most). View them in About → Trip logs and send one yourself with Share. Logs contain the measured locations as coordinates and never leave the device unless you share them.

About the app

  • Kaç Durak: Metrobüs, Metro (formerly called Metrobüs Kaç Durak). Covers Metrobüs, Marmaray, metro (M1A, M1B, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9, M11) and tram (T1, T4, T5) lines.
  • No route planning and no live vehicle positions; it only counts the stops remaining.
  • No account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases; the app is free.
  • Available for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play).
  • Turkish and English interface.
  • All processing happens on device; no internet needed.
